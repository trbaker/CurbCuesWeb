Caution. The directions and bearing in this app are determined from GPS and location data provided by Apple and the Open Street Map. Data may be incorrect or outdated. The utmost care should be taken when walking based on information returned by the app.

Privacy

Last updated 3 August 2026

The short version. CurbCues has no account and no sign-up. It does not track you, show ads, or sell anything. Your location is used to answer the question you pressed a button to ask, and it is not stored on any server run by us.

What CurbCues collects

Nothing is collected about you personally. There is no account, no email address, no advertising identifier, and no analytics package in the app.

How your location is used

When you press a button, the app reads your location and compass heading from your iPhone and uses them to look up an answer. That is the only reason location is requested, and it happens only while you are using the app. CurbCues does not use background location and does not record where you have been.

Your location is not written to a file, a database, or a server that we control. Once an announcement is spoken, the coordinates are gone.

Services the app talks to

To answer a question, CurbCues sends your approximate coordinates to two services. Both receive only the coordinates needed for that one lookup.

Apple

The Address and Food Nearby buttons use Apple’s geocoding and place search, built into iOS. Apple’s handling of those requests is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

OpenStreetMap

The Intersection button queries the Overpass API, a public service operated by volunteers of the OpenStreetMap project, to find nearby street corners. Your coordinates are sent as part of that query. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy.

What is stored on your iPhone

Two settings are saved on your device so they survive a restart: your Live update interval and your speech speed. They stay on the phone, are not sent anywhere, and are deleted when you delete the app.

Children

CurbCues is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects this policy, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change. If a future version were to collect anything, that would be described here before the version was released.

Contact

Questions about privacy in CurbCues can go to support@curbcues.app.
